Environmental epidemiology

Air pollution and birth outcomes

A study of how air pollution and maternal factors were associated with birth defects across U.S. counties from 2016 to 2023. The analysis combined national natality and air quality data with spatial analysis.

Presented at Texas A&M Student Research Week in 2025 and listed in the APHA 2025 Delta Omega poster session.
